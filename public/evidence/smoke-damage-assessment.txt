SMOKE DAMAGE ASSESSMENT — INC-2026-0148
East Wing server room — 6 October 2026

Prepared by: Amina Yusuf (Facilities)
Status: Preliminary — demo document for prototype review

1. SUMMARY
At approximately 09:52 a fire alarm was triggered in the East Wing server room.
An odour of burning and light smoke was observed near rack B-07. The affected
circuit was isolated and the room was ventilated.

2. OBSERVED DAMAGE
- Rack B-07 (row B): scorching on the top power distribution unit.
- Two patch cables (positions 14 and 15) show heat damage to the jacket.
- Adjacent racks B-06 and B-08 present no visible damage.

3. ACTIONS TAKEN
- Power to rack B-07 isolated at 10:06.
- Fire watch maintained for 60 minutes; no further smoke observed.
- Vendor engaged for a full electrical inspection.

4. RECOMMENDATION
Replace the rack B-07 PDU and affected cabling before returning the rack to
service. Re-test the fire panel loop after the replacement.
